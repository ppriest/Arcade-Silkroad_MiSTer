MAME 0.289, silkroad.cpp. scripts/write_timing.py: writes by scanline over N frames, MAME's
screen of 256 lines with vblank at 240-255 (silkroad.cpp:453-454; vblank start 240).
Runs: attract --skip 600 --frames 1800; play --coin 600 --skip 900 --frames 2400 (coin,
Start, P1 Right held, Button 1 pulsed; debug/wtiming/snap_play shows gameplay at the end).

Findings:
- Sprite RAM: written only on lines 240-249 (attract) and 240-243 (play), none in the
  visible area. 79-85% of writes in the first 2 lines after vblank start.
- Video registers 0xC0010C-0xC00123: only on lines 240-250 (attract), 240-243 (play).
- Tile VRAM: written during the visible area. Play: vram0 only on lines 27-75, vram1 on
  8-82; vram2 across the frame. Attract: all three across the frame.
- Palette: mostly in vblank (64-94%), the rest across the frame in both runs.

Consequence: MAME draws at vblank start from live RAM, which holds what the game wrote in
the previous vblank. Latching sprite RAM and the scroll registers once, after line 250 and
before line 0's prefetch, gives the same pairing per line. Tile VRAM and palette written
mid-frame show from the next line down on a per-line renderer, and in MAME from the next
frame: a known difference, checked in Phase 2 against MAME frames.
